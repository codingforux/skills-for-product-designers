# Pattern-scan ticket (AFK)

Runs in a subagent, with no user in the loop. The question is how other products solve the same problem.

1. **Claim** the ticket.
2. **Pick the products**: the ones the ticket names; otherwise five to eight, mixing direct competitors with products known for solving this problem well, from any sector.
3. **Gather the screens.** When the Mobbin MCP is connected, search its flows and screens for the problem, and save your picks to a Mobbin collection named after the map. Otherwise walk the live sites or apps with a browser tool. Link screens from the note; images stay in Mobbin or wherever they were captured.
4. **Write the findings note**:

   ```markdown
   # <ticket title>

   **Question:** <from the ticket>, **Collection:** <link, when there is one>

   ## Products

   ### <product>

   <how it solves the problem, step by step; one notable detail; links to the screens>

   ## Across products

   - **Convention:** <what most of them do, which users will expect>
   - **Worth stealing:** <the outliers that solve it better>
   - **Fit:** <what would and wouldn't suit this product, checked against the glossary and design system docs>
   ```

   Done when every picked product has an entry and Across products names the convention.

5. **Record** it: post the resolution comment (the gist, linking the note and the collection) and close the ticket.
