# Task ticket (AFK or HITL)

Legwork that must happen before a question can be answered: pulling numbers from an analytics tool, getting an export, getting access to a tool. It does rather than decides, and earns its place by unblocking a question.

1. **Claim** the ticket.
2. **Do it yourself** where your tools reach. Otherwise hand the user a **checklist** they can follow in a tool they may never have opened: where to go, what to click, what to look for, what to bring back and roughly how long it takes. Ask for **aggregates** (counts, rates, distributions, top tens), never customer records.
3. **Resolve** when the work is done. The findings note records what was done and the facts later tickets depend on: the numbers, the date range, where an export lives, who owns the data.
