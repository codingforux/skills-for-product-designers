# Research ticket (AFK)

Runs in a subagent, with no user in the loop.

1. **Claim** the ticket.
2. **Find the answer at the source that owns it**: a product's own site, help centre, changelog, pricing page, app store listing and reviews; a standard's own text; a study's paper rather than an article about it. Follow every claim back to its owner. Keep what a company says about itself ("reordering is easy") apart from what you observed or what its users report.
3. **Write the findings note**:

   ```markdown
   # <ticket title>

   **Question:** <from the ticket>

   ## Answer

   <a short paragraph>

   ## Evidence

   - <claim>: [source](link), accessed <date>. Observed, reported by users, or claimed by the company.

   ## Gaps

   <what public sources couldn't answer, and what would: an interview, a task>
   ```

   Done when every claim in the Answer has a source in Evidence, or the note shows the question can't be answered from public sources.

4. **Record** it: post the resolution comment (the gist, linking the note) and close the ticket. Propose any follow-up tickets from Gaps in your report back.
