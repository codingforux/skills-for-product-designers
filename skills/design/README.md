# Design

Skills for product design work, from a PM's PRD to reviewed designs in Figma.

## User-invoked

- [setup-design-skills](./setup-design-skills/SKILL.md): configure a product for these skills. Run once first.
- [grill-prd](./grill-prd/SKILL.md): interview you about a PM's PRD, sorting what's yours from what only your team can answer.
- [to-questionnaire](./to-questionnaire/SKILL.md): gather the open questions into one message for your team, then record the answers.
- [to-design-brief](./to-design-brief/SKILL.md): turn the conversation into a design brief on the tracker. Run it again with the brief's link to update it when the scope changes.
- [to-tickets](./to-tickets/SKILL.md): split a brief into thin tickets, each one you can design, review and hand off on its own.
- [implement-design-brief](./implement-design-brief/SKILL.md): design the whole brief in Figma with parallel subagents, one per ticket, then review it and fix the hard violations.
- [agent-retro](./agent-retro/SKILL.md): after a session, suggest improvements to the standards, design system docs, glossary or Figma MCP findings, and write the ones you approve.
- [ask-ryan](./ask-ryan/SKILL.md): ask which skill or flow fits your situation.

## Model-invoked

- [explore-variants](./explore-variants/SKILL.md): several radically different variants of a design in Figma, to pick a direction.
- [design-review](./design-review/SKILL.md): review Figma frames on three axes: System, Brief and Completeness.
- [figma-writes](./figma-writes/SKILL.md): the playbook for writing to Figma.
