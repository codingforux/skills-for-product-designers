# Research ticket

Answer the ticket's question from public sources, with no user in the loop.

1. **Go to the source that owns each fact**: a product's own site, help centre, changelog, pricing page and app store reviews; a standard's own text; a study's paper rather than an article about it.
2. **Label each claim**: observed by you, reported by users, or claimed by the company about itself.
3. **Post the finding**: the answer in a short paragraph, then each claim with its link and the date you read it, then the gaps: what public sources couldn't answer, and which ticket type would (an interview, a task).

Done when every claim in the answer has a source, or the finding shows the question can't be answered from public sources. Report the gaps as proposed tickets.
