# Pattern-scan ticket

Find how other products solve the ticket's problem, with no user in the loop.

1. **Pick the products**: the ones the ticket names; otherwise five to eight, mixing direct competitors with products known for solving this problem well, from any sector.
2. **Gather the screens.** With the Mobbin MCP connected, search its flows and screens, and save your picks to a collection named after the map. Otherwise walk the live sites with a browser tool. Link the screens; images stay where they were captured.
3. **Post the finding**: for each product, how it solves the problem step by step, with links. Then, across products, the **convention** (what most do, so users will expect it), what is **worth stealing** (the outliers that do it better) and the **fit** (what would and wouldn't suit this product, checked against its glossary and design system docs).

Done when every picked product is covered and the convention is named.
