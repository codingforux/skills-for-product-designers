# Team invites

Status: ux-brief

Source: briefs/team-invites-prd.md

## Problem

Workspace admins who want to add someone to their workspace have to email support, and it takes two days. New hires can't work until it's done. Field techs, who work on site from a phone, wait on the office before they can join.

## Goals and Success Metrics

- Admins add people to their workspace without waiting on support. Signal to watch: how many people are added by invite instead of by emailing support.
- New hires start work the same day. Signal to watch: time from invite sent to first sign-in, with 80% of new hires working in the workspace the same day.
- Field techs join from their phone without calling the office. Signal to watch: the share of invites accepted on a phone.

## Users

- **Workspace admin**: runs a team's workspace and decides who is in it. Needs to get a new hire working on day one.
- **Field tech**: works on site from a phone, often with gloves on and poor signal. Needs to join the right workspace without calling the office.

## User Stories

1. As a workspace admin, I want to invite one person to my workspace by email, so that they can start work without me emailing support
2. As a workspace admin, I want to invite several people at once, so that I can onboard a whole crew in one go
3. As a field tech, I want an email that takes me into the workspace I was invited to, so that I can join on site without calling the office
   - The email and the join flow work on a phone
   - The email names the workspace and who invited me
4. As a field tech, I want to join from my phone using the existing sign-in screens, so that joining feels familiar and I don't have to learn something new
   - An expired invite says it has expired and tells me to ask the admin for a new one
5. As a workspace admin, I want to see the invites that are still pending, so that I know who hasn't joined yet
   - Each pending invite shows who it was sent to, when it was sent, and when it expires
6. As a workspace admin, I want to cancel a pending invite, so that a wrong or unwanted invite can't be used
7. As a workspace admin, I want to resend an invite, so that a person who missed or lost the email can still join
   - Resending an expired invite starts a new 7 days
8. As a workspace admin, I want to see which invites have expired, so that I can resend them

## Requirements

### Functional

R1. An invite is sent by email to one person and is for one workspace
R2. An invite expires 7 days after it is sent
R3. Resending an invite, expired or not, starts a new 7 days
R4. An expired invite stays in the pending list, marked as expired, until an admin resends or cancels it
R5. A cancelled invite can no longer be used to join
R6. The product says "workspace" and "invite" throughout, never "organization", "org", "account" or "invitation link"

### Non-functional

N1. The invited person's flow is designed phone first, since field techs join from a phone on site
N2. The join flow reuses the existing sign-in screens
N3. Touch targets are large and the screens are readable in bright light, for field techs
N4. The join flow works on poor signal: it tells the person clearly when it can't reach the server and keeps what they typed
N5. The email is sent through the existing notification service, so the design works within its template

## Design Scope

The PRD items that are **yours** or **needs alignment**:

- Inviting people to a workspace by email, one or several at a time
- The email the invited person gets and joining from a phone
- Seeing pending invites, and cancelling or resending one
- Invite expiry (7 days, per the product manager)

For everything else the PRD covers and who owns it, see the [PRD snapshot](team-invites-prd.md).

## Out of Scope

- Single sign-on: legal review of the identity provider contract isn't finished
- Inviting people to more than one workspace at once: ruled out by the PRD for this release

## Open Questions

None.

Run **`/grill-prd`** first.

Give it the PRD by pasting it in, or pass a file, a doc link or a tracker issue. It interviews you to sharpen your understanding of the team invites PRD. It also does three other things:

- It reads only the personas the PRD affects.
- It marks each PRD item **yours**, **not yours** or **needs alignment**.
- It parks every question only your team can answer as a `needs-alignment` sub-issue of the brief.

**What comes next**

1. If anything got parked, run `/to-ux-questionnaire`. It gathers the open questions into one message for the PM. You send it, because the agent never messages anyone. If the PM replies with a new PRD draft instead of answers, re-run `/grill-prd`.
2. If nothing got parked, run `/to-ux-brief`. It publishes the `ux-brief` issue.
3. If the brief is bigger than one slice, run `/to-ux-tickets` and then `/implement-ux-brief`. Otherwise run `/implement-ux-brief` on the brief itself.

Keep steps 1–4 in one unbroken context window where you can, so the grilling, brief and tickets all build on the same thinking.

If this is your first flow on this product, run **`/setup-codingforux-skills`** once before `/grill-prd`. It checks that the Figma MCP can write, then sets up the UX folder, issue tracker, labels, Figma files, standards, glossary and personas the other skills assume.
